About this project

Welcome to this independent guide to the Cyber Poké Ball Ultra Ball.

This website documents gameplay mechanics, Pokémon locations, evolutions, Gym Leaders, the Pokémon League and other information discovered while playing and testing the Cyber Poké Ball Ultra Ball.

The goal of this project is to preserve detailed gameplay information that is difficult to find elsewhere online.
