About This Guide

This guide documents gameplay information discovered while playing the Cyber Poké Ball Ultra Ball version. The information includes Pokémon locations, evolution methods, Gym Leaders, the Pokémon League and specific gameplay observations.

Important: Some information in this guide is based on in-game observation and testing rather than official documentation. These observations are specifically identified where appropriate.

In-game observation: One Pichu was caught at level 23. After many battles against Pokémon around levels 2–4, it evolved into Pikachu after approximately two additional levels.

Feebas → Milotic

Feebas evolves into Milotic by gaining one level. Since Feebas is normally encountered around level 20, it can evolve after gaining its next level.

Skitty → Delcatty

Skitty can evolve into Delcatty after gaining one level. In one observed playthrough, Skitty evolved at level 21.

Clamperl → Gorebyss / Huntail

Clamperl has two possible evolutions in this game.

  • Gorebyss: Clamperl evolves at level 21 normally.
  • Huntail: Clamperl evolves at level 21 while not happy.

To obtain Huntail, Clamperl must gain its final level while having low happiness. A tested method is to use Clamperl as the lead Pokémon at the beginning of battles, switch it out for another Pokémon, and allow the second Pokémon to defeat the opponent. Clamperl participates in the beginning of the battle but does not defeat the opponent itself. This requires more battles for Clamperl to gain the required level.

Comparison: In the Poké Ball version, a similar mechanic occurs with the two possible evolutions of Gloom. Vileplume is obtained through normal evolution, while Bellossom is obtained when Gloom is not happy.

Nincada → Ninjask

Nincada evolves into Ninjask at level 10. Shedinja does not exist in these games.
